# Online Master in Industrial and Employment Relations 2026–2027

Public page for the **Online Master in Industrial and Employment Relations**, delivered by the International Training Centre of the ILO (ITCILO) in partnership with the University of Turin.

Live at <https://macielte-collab.github.io/mier-2026-2027-programme/>

A single self-contained `index.html` with no build step and no runtime dependencies.

## What this page shows

- The programme: background, objectives, the three phases and the methodology
- The year at a glance, and the seven macro-competence areas
- The full Phase 1 agenda: 115 sessions across 59 session days, 5 examinations and the break, colour-coded by area, searchable and filterable
- The confirmed faculty
- Seven self-guided ITCILO and ILO tools on the eCampus
- The thesis milestones, requirements and the seven assessment criteria

## Confirmed and unconfirmed sessions

A session is **confirmed** when its cell in the `Confirmed` column of the agenda workbook carries the green fill. 32 of the 115 sessions are confirmed.

A confirmed session publishes the name of its resource person, a **Join on Zoom** button and its own **Add to calendar** control, which downloads a single event. A session still being arranged shows its date, time, title, macro-competence area and mandatory reading, carries a **To be confirmed** tag and reads *Resource person to be confirmed*. It has no name, no join link and no calendar entry, and the name is absent from this repository rather than hidden by the interface.

## The Zoom room

Every confirmed session uses the same Zoom room, and the link is published on this page. The link carries the meeting passcode, so anyone holding this page's address can join a live session. The room should therefore run with a waiting room, and the passcode should be rotated if the page is taken down or its audience changes.

To change the room, edit `ZOOM_URL` and `ZOOM_ID` near the top of the script block in `index.html`; every place the link appears reads from that single pair.

## What is deliberately not here

This page is public, so it carries none of the following:

- The cohort. No student names, countries, employers or positions.
- The eLibrary. The mandatory readings are named in the agenda but the files are not published here; they are copyrighted works made available to enrolled students on the eCampus.

The complete dashboard, cohort panel and readings included, lives in a separate private repository and belongs behind the course login.

## Dates

The thesis outline is presented in the closing session of Phase 1 on **29 April 2027**. The thesis is completed by **2 July 2027**.

Course dates, fees and deadlines must always be confirmed against the live course page on itcilo.org before being quoted.
