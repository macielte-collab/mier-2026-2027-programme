# MIER 2026–2027 · Programme

Public programme page for the **Master in Industrial and Employment Relations**, delivered by the International Training Centre of the ILO (ITCILO) in partnership with the University of Turin.

Live at <https://macielte-collab.github.io/mier-2026-2027-programme/>

A single self-contained `index.html` with no build step and no runtime dependencies.

## What this page shows

- The programme: background, objectives, the three phases, methodology, fees and how to apply
- The year at a glance, and the seven macro-competence areas
- The full Phase 1 agenda: 115 sessions across 59 session days, 5 examinations and the break, colour-coded by area, searchable and filterable
- The confirmed faculty
- Seven self-guided ITCILO and ILO tools on the eCampus
- The thesis milestones, requirements and the seven assessment criteria

## Confirmed and unconfirmed sessions

A session is **confirmed** when its cell in the `Confirmed` column of the agenda workbook carries the green fill. 32 of the 115 sessions are confirmed.

A confirmed session publishes the name of its resource person. A session still being arranged shows its date, time, title, macro-competence area and mandatory reading, carries a **To be confirmed** tag and reads *Resource person to be confirmed*. Its resource person is not named, and the name is absent from this repository rather than hidden by the interface.

The calendar export covers the confirmed sessions only.

## What is deliberately not here

This page is public, so it carries none of the following:

- The cohort. No participant names, countries, employers or positions.
- The Zoom room. No link and no meeting ID. Enrolled participants join from the ITCILO eCampus.
- The eLibrary. The mandatory readings are named in the agenda but the files are not published here; they are copyrighted works made available to enrolled participants on the eCampus.

The complete dashboard, cohort panel and readings included, lives in a separate private repository and belongs behind the course login.

## Dates

The thesis outline is presented in the closing session of Phase 1 on **29 April 2027**. The thesis is completed by **2 July 2027**.

Course dates, fees and deadlines must always be confirmed against the live course page on itcilo.org before being quoted.
